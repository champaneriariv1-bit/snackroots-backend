What Happens When You Eat Processed Snacks Daily
Your body responds to ultra-processed foods in stages. Here's the timeline most people don't know about.
Day 1
Blood sugar spikes 30–40% above baseline within 30 minutes of eating chips or soda. Energy crash follows in 90 minutes.
Week 1
Gut microbiome diversity begins to decrease. Inflammatory markers rise subtly. Skin may feel more oily.
Month 1
Habitual cravings form as dopamine response to junk food intensifies. Sleep quality noticeably worsens due to late-night snacking cycles.
Month 3
Insulin sensitivity drops. Risk of metabolic syndrome increases. Cognitive focus and mood regulation become harder to sustain.
Year 1+
Compounding effects: higher cardiovascular risk, potential weight gain, reduced nutrient absorption capacity, weakened immune response.

Our Story

Why We Built SnackRoots

🌏 Built for a Healthier Planet
SnackRoots was created to bridge the gap between what food companies tell you about their snacks and what the science actually shows. We believe awareness is the first step to change.
12M+

Carbon neutral platform since 2024
"You can't make better choices if you don't know the real cost of what you're eating."

SnackRoots was born from a simple frustration: food labels are designed to confuse, not inform. We wanted to build a tool that respects your intelligence and gives you the full picture — calories, carbon, chemicals, and all.

Our platform combines nutritional science, environmental data, and food system research to give every snack a honest, comprehensive rating. No greenwashing. No misleading "health" claims.

We believe that individual food choices — multiplied across billions of people — have the power to reshape our food systems for the better. One snack swap at a time.
