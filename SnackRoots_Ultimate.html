🌍 Sustainable Sourcing

Snacking with a conscience.
Because duh.

We believe that snacks should taste like a rebellion against boring food, but they absolutely shouldn't damage the planet in the process. Here's how we keep things clean, green, and gloriously tasty.

🌱

Locally Sourced Heroes

We raid local farms before they even wake up. Less fuel, more flavor. SnackRoots sources ingredients from nearby regions to cut down food miles and support regional farmers. This isn't just smart economics — it's a massive carbon footprint reducer.

♻️

Trash-Talking Not Allowed

Our wrappers break down faster than a bad Netflix show. SnackRoots uses sustainable packaging designed to reduce landfill waste. Recyclable, compostable, and minimal plastic. Because nothing kills a snack vibe faster than a 400-year-old wrapper.

🌍

Plants, Plants, More Plants

The only gas here is the excited kind. Plant-based snacks = lighter impact on the planet. We skip the heavy industrial processing and the factory smoke. Just plants doing parkour with your appetite and your metabolism.

⚡

Small-Batch Energy

Our machines are tiny, our emissions are tinier. We use small-batch production instead of giant industrial machinery. Less energy consumption equals fewer emissions. It's like artisanal baking, but for globally-sourced crunch-fests.

🚫

Zero Waste, Full Belly

Food waste contributes heavily to methane emissions. Sadness. We use whole ingredients and repurpose what's left. If it's edible and safe, it gets turned into something genius. Every last crunchy bit gets a chance to shine.

🌿

Clean Label, Clear Conscience

Ingredients so clean you could read them to a nun. Less chemical processing = less industrial pollution. We keep our labels distressingly transparent. No fake stuff, no cryptic codes — just pure, honest, gentle-on-the-earth snacking.

Quick Answers

FAQ

How long does delivery take?

Delivery time changes by PIN code. The checkout checks the India Post PIN database when possible, then shows an estimated date range for that destination.

Are allergens shown?

Yes. Use the filters and product detail modal. Customers with serious allergies should still check the final pack label.

Does the cart save after refresh?

Yes. The cart and coupon are stored in browser storage on this device until checkout or manual removal.

How does the WWF contribution work?

The site estimates 2% of each order for a World Wildlife Fund contribution record. Actual live donation sending needs the server and verified WWF API settings.

Can I order on WhatsApp?

Yes. The WhatsApp Cart button opens a message with the current cart summary.

Privacy & Cookies

Clear rules for customer data

SnackRoots only asks for information needed to recommend snacks, process orders, protect the creator dashboard, and maintain the 2% World Wildlife Fund contribution record.

What we collect

  • Name, email, phone, and delivery address during checkout.
  • Cart items, coupon code, order total, payment ID, and order status.
  • Creator admin key only for the current browser session.

How it is used

Order details are used to pack, deliver, and manage SnackRoots purchases. Impact records are used to calculate and track the estimated 2% World Wildlife Fund contribution amount attached to each order.

Cookies and local storage

The site uses essential browser storage for cart behavior, creator login session, pending order recovery, cookie preference, and the embedded food impact tool. It does not add advertising trackers.

Security promise

Admin routes require a creator key on the server. Private `.env` values are not included in the public HTML or zip. API keys should be rotated if they are ever shared outside the server environment.
